===== SECTION 1 =====
Section title: What it is

[IMAGE: docs/logo/ac-dlssg-banner.png]

[h1]NVIDIA Frame Generation in Assetto Corsa[/h1]

[b]ac-dlssg[/b] is a free mod that adds [b]NVIDIA DLSS Frame Generation[/b] to Assetto Corsa, the same frame generation that new games have.

In plain words: your graphics card renders a real frame and inserts generated frames between the real ones. You get 2, 3 or 4 times as many frames on screen, and the picture moves more smoothly.

[list]
[*][b]2X, 3X and 4X[/b], chosen in game.
[*][b]NVIDIA Reflex[/b] turns on by itself and keeps input latency low.
[*][b]An in-game settings window[/b]: on/off, multiplier, FPS, video memory.
[*][b]One-click install[/b]: one install.bat, no questions asked.
[*][b]Works on laptops[/b], even with 4 GB of video memory.
[*][b]Free and open source[/b] on GitHub.
[/list]

Why: you can run heavier graphics (weather, traffic, shadows, supersampling) and still get a smooth picture.

===== SECTION 3 =====
Section title: What you need

[h1]What you need[/h1]

[list]
[*]An [b]NVIDIA RTX 30, 40 or 50[/b] graphics card (laptop GPUs work too).
[*][b]Assetto Corsa[/b] on Steam and a [b]Custom Shaders Patch preview build[/b] (0.3.0-preview or newer), published by CSP's author on Patreon: [url=https://www.patreon.com/c/x4fab/home]patreon.com/c/x4fab[/url]
[*][b]DLSS[/b] selected as the upscaler in CSP's settings (in Content Manager: Settings, Custom Shaders Patch, search for "DLSS").
[*]Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) or Windows 11, with "Hardware-accelerated GPU scheduling" on.
[*]An internet connection during install: the installer downloads NVIDIA's files by itself.
[/list]

[b]RTX 30:[/b] NVIDIA officially enables frame generation only on RTX 40 and newer. On RTX 30 the installer downloads the third-party project [url=https://github.com/sdli1995/dlssg_for_sm86]dlssg_for_sm86[/url] (by sdli1995), which lifts that limit. It is not an NVIDIA project, and it works around NVIDIA's restriction. If you do not want it, the installer has a -NoSpoof option.


===== SECTION 4 =====
Section title: Install (5 minutes)

[h1]Install[/h1]

[olist]
[*][b]Close the game.[/b]
[*][b]Download[/b] ac-dlssg-1.0.0.zip from the latest release on GitHub:
[url=https://github.com/flopsy2iqq/ac-dlssg/releases/latest]github.com/flopsy2iqq/ac-dlssg/releases[/url]
[*][b]Unpack it[/b] (right-click, "Extract All").
[*][b]Double-click install.bat.[/b] If Windows asks "Allow changes?", click Yes. Wait until it says it is done, then press Enter.
[*][b]Start the game[/b] as usual and drive. Frame generation turns on by itself.
[/olist]

The installer finds the game, downloads NVIDIA's files, installs dlssg_for_sm86 on RTX 30, and asks nothing. It works with or without ReShade.

Step-by-step guide: [url=https://github.com/flopsy2iqq/ac-dlssg/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md]install guide[/url]


===== SECTION 5 =====
Section title: How to use it

[h1]How to use it[/h1]

[list]
[*]Open the [b]AC DLSS-G[/b] window from CSP's app bar. It has the frame generation switch, the [b]2X / 3X / 4X[/b] buttons, the real and output FPS, the video memory and a "Save as default" button.
[*][b]Ctrl+F10[/b] switches frame generation on and off, for a quick comparison.
[*]Frame generation turns itself off in the pause menu and the main menu.
[*]CSP's FPS counter shows only the real frames. Use the NVIDIA overlay (Alt+R) or RTSS to see the frames on screen.
[/list]

[IMAGE: a screenshot of the AC DLSS-G window]

[b]Tip:[/b] 2X gives the cleanest picture, 3X and 4X give the most smoothness. Frame generation works best when you have at least 35-40 FPS without it.


===== SECTION 6 =====
Section title: FAQ

[h1]FAQ[/h1]

[b]What about input lag?[/b]
The mod turns on NVIDIA Reflex by itself. Latency is a little higher than without frame generation, but it was not noticeable on a wheel or a gamepad in testing.

[b]My FPS did not go up.[/b]
Open the AC DLSS-G window: it says why frame generation is off. Most often DLSS is not selected in CSP, or on a laptop the game runs on the integrated GPU (Windows Settings, System, Display, Graphics: set acs.exe to High performance).

[b]Not much video memory (4 GB)?[/b]
The mod adapts to the card by itself. When memory is tight, the window tells you what to lower (textures, shadows).

[b]Any artifacts?[/b]
In fast motion, CSP app windows and on-screen UI can smear a little, because the whole frame is generated. The road and the cars stay clean.

[b]RTX 20, GTX, AMD?[/b]
No, it needs an RTX 30, 40 or 50.

[b]ReShade? Online?[/b]
It works with ReShade. The mod only changes how the frame is shown on screen and does not touch the game logic.

[b]How do I uninstall it?[/b]
In the unpacked folder, open tools and double-click uninstall.bat. Everything goes back to how it was.
